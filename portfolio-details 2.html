Project Overview

Performed comprehensive data cleaning and transformation using Power Query to ensure data accuracy and consistency. Built optimized DAX Measures and calculated metrics to analyze key performance indicators, then designed an interactive Power BI dashboard with clear visualizations, filters, and charts that enable efficient exploration of BMW sales and pricing data.

Analyzed the dataset to identify hidden patterns and uncover the root cause behind the decline in **2023 sales**. By examining the relationship between marketing expenditure and sales performance, I found a strong positive correlation indicating that reduced marketing investment was a key factor contributing to the sales drop. Based on these findings, I recommended increasing and maintaining marketing investment over a longer period to improve campaign effectiveness, support sustainable sales growth, and enable more reliable performance evaluation.

Key Features

Data Preparation

Cleaned, transformed, and validated the BMW dataset using Power Query to ensure accurate and reliable analysis.

Performance Analysis

Tracked sales, revenue, pricing, and marketing KPIs.

Root Cause Analysis

Discovered that reduced marketing investment was strongly associated with the decline in 2023 sales.

Business Recommendations

Recommended maintaining a long-term marketing strategy and increasing marketing investment to support sustainable sales growth.
